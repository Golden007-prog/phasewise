# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background or experience. Be kind,
be patient, and keep discussions about the work.

## Expected

- Assume good intent and ask before you judge.
- Give feedback on the change and its effect, never on the person.
- Disagree clearly and calmly, with reasons.
- Keep private what was shared with you in private.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Deliberately derailing issues, discussions or pull requests.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.

## Reporting

Open an issue asking a maintainer for a private contact, without names or
details. Once you have a private channel, report the problem there.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this code and may
block those who keep doing so.
